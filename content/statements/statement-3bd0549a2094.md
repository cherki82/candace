### Analysis

The broad claim overstates the record. The State filing says the rifle DNA report was produced in October 2025, and the defense ballistics motion says the ATF report was disclosed on December 18, 2025. The defense still sought underlying forensic files and independent access to physical evidence, so lack of complete access was partly true, but it had not been wholly unable to inspect all three categories. Robinson remains accused and has not been convicted.
