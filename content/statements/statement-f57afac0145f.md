### Analysis

By Way of Deception did allege that Mossad had advance intelligence about a large Mercedes truck being prepared for an attack and passed only a general warning to the United States. The bombing occurred in October 1983, not 1982; the book did not conclusively establish the exact attack time, and Israeli officials publicly denied the allegation.
