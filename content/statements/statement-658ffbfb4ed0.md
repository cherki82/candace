### Analysis

The ATF examiner did provide a non-exclusive list of possible .30-caliber firearms, but the recovered Mauser .30-06 was not excluded. Her ultimate conclusion was inconclusive: the fragment could not be identified as or excluded from having been fired by that rifle. The claim that the possible-weapon range did not include a .30-06 is therefore directly contradicted by the sworn testimony.
