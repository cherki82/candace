### Analysis

The DOJ memo limits FARA and section 951 criminal charges to alleged conduct resembling traditional espionage by foreign government actors and shifts the FARA Unit toward civil enforcement. That broadly reduces criminal exposure for ordinary advocacy and public-relations work, but the memo does not categorically immunize all PR or policy advocacy for foreign businesses and nonprofits.
