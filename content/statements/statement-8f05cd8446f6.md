### Analysis

Official sources confirm Timpanogos is in HCA’s MountainStar system and Intermountain uses Epic. HCA identifies its EHR as MEDITECH Expanse, not “VDI,” and Intermountain states it participates in Epic Care Everywhere and can request and receive patient records. Thus the ownership portions are supported but the named HCA system and categorical claim that the hospitals do not share or exchange patient information are contradicted.
