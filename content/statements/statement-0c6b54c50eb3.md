### Analysis

The FBI confirms it conducted bullet-lead examinations from the early 1980s and formally discontinued them in September 2005 after an outside review and internal study. The National Research Council found the analytical instrumentation precise but raised serious limits on interpretation and evidentiary significance. Thus the timing and abandonment are broadly right, but saying the method was abandoned simply because its scientific, statistical, and interpretive validity were all flawed overstates the official findings.
