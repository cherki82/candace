### Analysis

The 70-to-80-percent figure is supported by older national BJS data only when analysis is limited to homicides where the victim-offender relationship was known: 73 to 79 percent from 1993 through 2008. Current 2024 FBI NIBRS reporting classifies 35.2 percent as known nonfamily, 14.8 percent as family, 0.8 percent as family and other, 16.5 percent as stranger, and 32.7 percent as all other or unknown. The unqualified present-tense claim therefore omits an important denominator and time-period qualification.
