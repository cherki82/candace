### Analysis

FoldAR official documentation confirms tool-free barrel-system changes in seconds. NIST explains that a barrel leaves microscopic marks on a fired bullet and that examiners compare a suspect gun test fire to the crime bullet. Replacing the fired barrel can therefore prevent a crime bullet from matching test bullets fired through the replacement barrel. This does not eliminate cartridge-case marks, DNA, fingerprints, or other forensic links, so the broader claim of being scot-free would be overstated but is not part of this statement.
