### Analysis

Current Utah sources support that evidence release generally requires authorization and that a prosecuting attorney may authorize or petition for release in some circumstances. The claim that only a district attorney can authorize every release is too broad: a Utah police evidence policy also permits authorization by the responsible officer, and state law assigns some transfers to a district court on a signed prosecutorial petition.
