## Statement

> Sketchers made more detailed drawings when distractors were close to the target category and sparser drawings when category-level abstractions were sufficient.

> Using this really simple manipulation, we discovered how readily ordinary folks can adjust the way they use depiction to communicate, making more detailed and faithful drawings on those close trials when they needed their drawing to uniquely identify a particular exemplar, but then sparser drawings on the far trials when they could get away with these category-level abstractions.
