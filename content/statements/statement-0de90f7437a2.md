### Analysis

Transcript context reviewed. The hexagram did not become a broadly recognized Jewish emblem until the early modern period, but its association with Jewish communities predates the seventeenth century. The Prague Jewish community was granted a flag bearing the symbol in 1354, and medieval synagogue and manuscript uses are documented. The categorical 'not associated until' claim is therefore false.
