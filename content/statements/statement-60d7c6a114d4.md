### Analysis

Reliable histories confirm that Falcone and Gaydamak brokered large arms shipments from Eastern Europe, including through Slovak supplier ZTS-OSOS and Falcone's Brenco network, to Angola during the 1990s. But Global Witness dates the public emergence of Angolagate to the end of 2000, not French elections in 1999, and the record describes private brokers operating from France rather than a demonstrated French-government program trafficking the weapons. The network description is substantially supported; the discovery timing and characterization of the French government overstate the evidence.
