### Analysis

Official CISA, DOE, and GAO materials confirm serious grid risks from cyberattackers, physical attacks, solar geomagnetic disturbance, and man-made EMP. However, hackers are actors within the cyber category rather than a distinct threat class, and the unqualified phrase extremely vulnerable overstates unsettled estimates of severity, especially for solar and high-altitude EMP effects.
