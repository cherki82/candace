### Analysis

The project architect confirms Bracebridge was an MBNA headquarters campus of more than 1 million square feet, built in 1995-1997 and passing to Bank of America with its 2006 acquisition of MBNA. Contemporary reporting confirms Bank of America sold Bracebridge I and III in 2018 but retained another Wilmington building, so “left it completely empty” overstates the disposition of the whole complex. The parenthetical claim that Macron came to power in 2018 is also wrong; he became president in 2017. Core property history true, qualifiers mixed.
