### Analysis

A 2022 umbrella review found no consistent evidence that depression is caused by low serotonin, supporting rejection of the simplistic chemical-imbalance story. However, SSRIs do have an established proximal pharmacologic action: they inhibit neuronal serotonin reuptake. The full downstream therapeutic mechanism and delayed clinical effect remain incompletely understood, and published researchers have disputed the umbrella review methodology. Thus the claim is directionally right about the causal imbalance narrative but too broad about what SSRIs do.
