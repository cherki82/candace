### Analysis

Contemporaneous and later reporting supports that the Douglas tunnel was a major cocaine route and that its discovery produced public suspicion of compromised or collaborating U.S. officials; several border agents were later convicted in other local cases. Official DOJ testimony says about one ton of cocaine moved through the 1990 tunnel. However, the reviewed sources do not support the specific assertion that 60 to 80 percent of cocaine entered through the Douglas area or document the exact claimed response by local authorities. The suspicion component is supported, while the numerical and attribution components are not.
