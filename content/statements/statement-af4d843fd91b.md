### Analysis

The unqualified claim that Jewish people controlled the slave trade is directly contradicted by the historical record summarized by the American Historical Association and by quantitative scholarship. The AHA states that Jews could play no more than a marginal role in the vast Atlantic system and condemns as false claims of disproportionate Jewish control. Eli Faber's archival study likewise finds exceedingly limited participation. Jewish individuals did participate in slavery and slave trading in some places, but that does not support group control of the trade.
