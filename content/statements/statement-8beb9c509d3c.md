### Analysis

Public trademark records show three CHARLIE KIRK applications owned by Erika Kirk, serial numbers 99427865, 99427866, and 99427868, all filed October 4, 2025, 24 days after the September 10 death. The applications include tote bags and clothing. However, filing applications did not itself grant registered rights, and the records show applications rather than completed registrations, so the statement overstates their legal effect.
