### Analysis

Verified as a characterization of Jeffrey Masson’s thesis, not as proof that every abuse report was historically true. Masson argues Freud suppressed or reclassified accounts of childhood sexual abuse; later scholarship sharply disputes parts of Masson’s reconstruction. The legacy wording about “covering for parents raping toddlers” was broader and more inflammatory than the cited thesis and has been narrowed.
