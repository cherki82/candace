### Analysis

Transcript context confirms Owens made a broad claim that the CIA trafficked both drugs and weapons throughout South America during Iran-Contra. Current official reviews support a narrower account: the CIA and other U.S. officials participated in the Iran arms operation and supported the Contras, while investigations documented drug trafficking by some Contra supporters and questions about U.S. officials' handling of those allegations. The Justice Department inspector general did not substantiate that the CIA itself operated the drug trafficking described. The statement therefore conflates documented arms activity and knowledge of Contra-linked trafficking with direct CIA drug trafficking, and also overstates the geography.
