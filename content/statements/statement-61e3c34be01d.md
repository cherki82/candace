### Analysis

There is affirmative official evidence that MKULTRA ended: CIA historical material says drug experiments were terminated in 1967 and later indirect support was ended in 1973; a House Oversight hearing likewise describes the project as completed and its records as largely destroyed. Whether later unlawful programs existed is a different claim.
