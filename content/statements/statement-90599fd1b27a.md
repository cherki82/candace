### Analysis

The claim identifies Ashkenazi Jews as a Turkic population from Turkey and contrasts that with ancestry from the Holy Land. Genome-wide research directly contradicts that simple origin account: large studies find Ashkenazi ancestry deriving principally from Middle Eastern and European populations, with no indication of a significant Khazar contribution, and later work finds both European and Middle Eastern components while disqualifying wholly Turkish or Khazar models. The rhetorical phrase 'way more proof' is not a measurable standard, but its underlying ancestry assertion is contradicted by the cited genetic evidence.
