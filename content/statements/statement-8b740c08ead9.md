### Analysis

False with medium confidence. Episode context and the preserved legacy history identify the supposed Ukrainian list as Myrotvorets. NewsGuard’s search of that database, reproduced in AI Incident Database report 6208, found no evidence that Charlie Kirk ever appeared and returned no result for his name; the report traces the contrary claim to a false AI-generated summary. PolitiFact separately explains that Myrotvorets is a database of people its operators deem hostile to Ukraine, not a literal state hit list, and an entry does not itself call for killing or harm. Direct access to Myrotvorets was blocked during this review and a removed historical entry cannot be ruled out absolutely, so confidence is medium rather than high. The episode verifies that Owens made the claim, not that the proposition is true. The earlier legacy mixed assessment remains preserved in legacy_claim_provenance.
