### Analysis

Utah Code 76-8-510.5 confirms that knowingly altering, destroying, concealing, or removing evidence with the purpose of impairing its veracity or availability can constitute evidence tampering. It is ordinarily a class A misdemeanor and becomes a third-degree felony only when committed in conjunction with an official proceeding. The episode's legal proposition is therefore possible but incomplete: removal or potential alteration by itself does not establish the required purpose, and the public record reviewed does not establish that Farnsworth satisfied the felony conditions.
