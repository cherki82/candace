### Analysis

Attorney General Bondi’s February 5, 2025 memorandum did disband the Foreign Influence Task Force. But it did not eliminate the FARA Unit: the same memo directed the Counterintelligence and Export Control Section, including the FARA Unit, to focus on civil enforcement, regulatory initiatives, and public guidance. The statement is correct on disbandment but wrong that the DOJ office responsible for FARA was eliminated.
