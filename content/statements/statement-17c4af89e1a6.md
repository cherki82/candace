### Analysis

Several components are documented: Kennedy rode in an open limousine, the vice president rode in a separate car, and the motorcade slowed for the Houston-to-Elm turn. But the Warren Commission says the motorcade followed the planned route, that the route had been publicized before the assassination, and that the limousine maintained an average 11.2 mph over the 186 feet immediately preceding the fatal head shot. Calling the route a last-minute change and saying the vehicle slowed specifically at the Depository are not supported by these records. The numeric claim of 15 or 20 inexcusable events is evaluative and not independently verifiable.
