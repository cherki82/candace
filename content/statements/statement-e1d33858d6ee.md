### Analysis

Transcript asserts that the museum/relic site was the first destination and base of U.S. forces. Current institutional records show Camp Alpha was established at Babylon after the invasion, but do not show it was the first place U.S. forces went; the Gilgamesh work was at Uruk, a different site. The composite claim is false.
