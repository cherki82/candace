### Analysis

The New York Times article reported anonymous Israeli defense officials' estimate that Gaza's tunnel network was 350 to 450 miles, while explicitly stating that the numbers could not be independently verified. The available full reprint does not itself compare that estimate with the New York City subway. MTA statistics list 248 nondirectional route miles but 665 track miles, so 'longer than the subway system' depends on the metric and overstates the article's actual wording.
