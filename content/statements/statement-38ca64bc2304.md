### Analysis

The quoted post existed, and Owens was selling Candace Intelligence Agency merchandise. However, 50 U.S.C. 3513 prohibits commercial use only when reasonably calculated to convey CIA approval and specifies a civil injunction remedy; it does not make every unlicensed parody or imitation a federal felony. The merchandise fact is true, while the categorical felony claim is not supported by the cited statute.
