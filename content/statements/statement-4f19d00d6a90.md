### Analysis

The recorded Twiggs interview played at the preliminary hearing expressly warns that false statements can be punished, including as a misdemeanor or obstruction, immediately before acknowledging federal and state immunity agreements (Day 4, approximately 02:11–02:14). These directly contradict the blanket claim that immunity allowed lying without punishment.
