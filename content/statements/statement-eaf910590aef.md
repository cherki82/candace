### Analysis

Current reporting supports severe interceptor depletion, but the 80 percent figure applies to the THAAD stockpile used during the Iran war, while about half of Patriot interceptors were used. It is inaccurate to generalize 80 percent to all U.S. missile interceptors or attribute that full share jointly to Israel, Ukraine, and Iran.
